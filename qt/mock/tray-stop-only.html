Menu bar + recording region · recording state

Just the stop button. A softer red box.

Remove the elapsed-time pill from the tray. While a recording is running, Snapforge keeps a single menu-bar slot — the red stop square. Left-click stops immediately; right-click opens the recording menu so Pause, History, Preferences and Quit stay reachable. The region outline gets the same treatment: a 1px translucent red hairline with soft corners and no fill, instead of a hard square box. The recorded click ripple follows: an 8px dot with a small white center and one thin ring, not a heavy 52px halo.

Click effect · recorded ripple

The current marker is a heavy 52px ring with a solid red dot and a white core. Keep the same red dot, shrink the white center, and use one thin ring that expands with an ease-out curve. Visible in the recording, quiet over the content.

Right-click keeps every control reachable

Hiding the main tray item during recording removes the only way to pause or quit. The stop button keeps that path: a plain click stops, a right-click pops the recording menu.

  • Left-clickStop the recording immediately.
  • Right-clickPause / resume, stop, history, preferences, quit.
